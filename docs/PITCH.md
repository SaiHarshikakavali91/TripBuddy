# 3-minute live demo script

**0:00 — Problem**
“Travel planning is fragmented. A traveller jumps between flights, hotels, blogs, maps and group chats. TripBuddy puts the whole journey into one agentic workspace.”

**0:25 — Create**
“I'm travelling to Andhra Pradesh. I want heritage, food and nature, a controlled budget and a relaxed pace. My friends have different priorities.”

**0:45 — Agent run**
“TripBuddy first builds my Travel DNA, then optimises the route, checks the budget and mediates group preferences before generating the plan.”

**1:15 — Itinerary**
“Instead of a generic Top-10 list, the itinerary is structured by day, geography, budget and pace.”

**1:40 — Self-healing**
“Now imagine a delay. I trigger self-heal. The agent removes the disrupted activity, shifts the route and preserves the high-priority experiences.”

**2:05 — Group Hub**
“Each traveller gets a preference card. The mediation engine finds the overlap instead of forcing one person's plan.”

**2:25 — Budget**
“Group spending is transparent. The product can prepare a UPI-style split, so each member can see their share.”

**2:45 — Close**
“TripBuddy is not another inventory listing screen. It is an intelligent travel operating layer that plans, executes and adapts.”
